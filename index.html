About Us

Welcome to OyisaTech 🚀
Building Digital Solutions. Turning Ideas into Reality.

OyisaTech is a South African technology brand founded by Oyisa Landu, with a vision to make technology more accessible to businesses, entrepreneurs, and individuals.

We believe that every great idea deserves an opportunity to grow. Our goal is to help turn ideas into practical digital solutions that make businesses more accessible, efficient, and ready for the future.

Our Story

OyisaTech was founded from a passion for technology, creativity, and innovation. As a self-taught app creator, Oyisa Landu began exploring how mobile technology and digital platforms could solve everyday problems and create new opportunities.

One of the projects developed from this journey is SA Opportunity Hub by Oyisa, a platform created to help people discover opportunities.

This project represents an important step in our journey: learning, building, improving, and turning ideas into reality.

We believe that you do not need to start with a big office or a large team to begin building something meaningful. What matters is having a vision, the willingness to learn, and the determination to keep moving forward.

What We Aim to Do

  • Website Development: Creating websites that help businesses establish an online presence.
  • Mobile App Development: Exploring and developing mobile applications designed around real-world needs.
  • E-commerce Solutions: Helping businesses explore ways to showcase and sell their products online.
  • Digital Business Solutions: Turning business ideas into practical digital experiences.
  • UI/UX Design: Focusing on user-friendly designs that make digital products easier to use.

Our available services depend on the project requirements and our current development capabilities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to use technology and creativity to help businesses and individuals bring their ideas to life through practical, accessible, and user-friendly digital solutions.

Our Vision

Our vision is to grow OyisaTech into a trusted South African technology brand known for innovation, reliability, creativity, and solutions that make a difference.

Why OyisaTech?

We believe in starting small, learning continuously, and building with purpose.

Every project is an opportunity to improve, solve problems, and create something valuable. We aim to approach our work with honesty, dedication, and a commitment to continuous growth.
